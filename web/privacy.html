Privacy & legal

Last updated: 21 November 2026

In short: this site does not collect, store or share personal data. No cookies, no analytics, no tracking, no ads, no accounts. Your settings stay on your own device. Only if you press “Check live now” does your browser contact the companies’ status pages directly.

1. Who runs this site

“Is It Just Me?” is a private, non-commercial open-source project by the GitHub user CodeMitVishal. Contact: open an issue at github.com/CodeMitVishal/is-it-just-me/issues (please don’t post personal data there).

2. Hosting (GitHub Pages)

When you open this site, your browser connects to servers of GitHub, Inc. (USA). To deliver the site and protect it from abuse, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address, the time and the page requested (server logs). We have no access to these logs.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — legitimate interest in delivering this website securely. GitHub is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. Details: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

3. What is stored on your device

The site uses your browser’s local storage (not cookies) only to remember choices you make:

KeyPurpose
theme, font, langYour display choices
followsThe services you follow (bell)
lastSeenTheir last known status, to tell you what changed since your last visit

This data never leaves your device and is not used for tracking. It is strictly necessary to provide functions you request (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG; Art. 5(3) ePrivacy Directive), so no consent banner is needed. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

4. Notifications

Notifications appear only if you allow them in your browser. They are created locally by your browser while the page is open. There is no push server, and we cannot contact you when the page is closed.

5. No third parties by default

All fonts, icons and scripts are served from this site. Your browser does not contact Google Fonts, content delivery networks, analytics or advertising services. Normally the status data is collected automatically and saved on this site, so your browser does not contact the companies.

“Check live now” (only if you press it): your browser then asks the public status pages of the listed companies directly (for example status.zoom.us or githubstatus.com) for their current status. Like any website visit, those companies receive your IP address and browser details; their own privacy policies apply. No cookies or personal data from this site are sent, and no referrer. Pressing the button is your choice (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); if you don’t press it, nothing is sent. The allowed addresses are fixed in the page’s security policy.

6. Links to other websites

Links to official status pages open the company’s own website; their privacy policy applies there. We do not send them where you came from (no referrer).

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object (Art. 15–21). Because we hold no personal data, there is usually nothing for us to provide; for GitHub’s server logs please contact GitHub. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR).

8. Logos and trademarks

Product names and logos belong to their respective owners. They are shown only to identify each service and were taken from each company’s public status page. Their use does not imply any affiliation with or endorsement by those companies. Rights holders who want a logo removed can open an issue and it will be removed promptly.

9. Accessibility

The site aims to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA: full keyboard use, screen-reader labels, status shown by shape and text (not colour alone), a high-contrast theme, an easy-to-read font and reduced motion when your device asks for it. Found a barrier? Please open an issue.

10. Disclaimer

Information comes from public status pages and may be delayed or incomplete. It is provided without warranty. For important decisions, check the company’s official status page.
